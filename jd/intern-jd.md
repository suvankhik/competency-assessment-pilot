XYZ LOGISTICS PVT. LTD. - JOB SPECIFICATION & DESCRIPTION

PART I: Job Identification & Organizational Context
- Job Title: Intern - Supply Chain & Logistics Operations
- **Position Code:** OPS-SCM-INT-01
- **Department:** Logistics / Supply Chain Operations
- **Location:** Distribution Hub (Pilot Case Study)
- **Reporting To:** Logistics Operations Manager
- **Direct Subordinates:** None
- **Employment Status:** Internship, 2 months, non-permanent

**PART II: Enterprise Overview & Role Summary**
- **Enterprise Overview:** XYZ Logistics is a mid-sized logistics and warehousing company focused on timely, cost-efficient delivery and inventory accuracy.
- **Role Summary:** The intern supports the operations team in tracking shipments, maintaining inventory records and assisting with planning, while learning day-to-day supply chain operations under supervision.

**PART III: Core Duties & Key Result Areas (KRAs)**
1. **Shipment & Order Tracking:** update daily trackers, flag delays to the supervisor
2. **Inventory & Warehouse Support:** assist stock counts, record discrepancies in Excel
3. **Planning Support:** help prepare simple dispatch and route schedules
4. **Reporting & Cost Awareness:** prepare weekly summaries of delays, costs and delivery performance
5. **Team Coordination:** communicate with team members, drivers and vendors, and attend daily handovers
6. **Learning & Compliance:** follow safety and documentation norms, and complete assigned learning tasks

**PART IV: Formal Job Specifications (Minimum Requirements)**
1. **Education:** pursuing MBA in Logistics and Supply Chain Management (Mandatory)
2. **Prior Experience:** None required. Academic projects or college activities are considered
3. **Technical Proficiencies:** basic Excel (formulas, tables), basic supply chain concepts (inventory, transportation, lead time)
4. **Behavioral Competencies:** supply chain awareness, planning and organizing, teamwork and communication, learning agility
