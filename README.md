# Competency Assessment Mapping (Ongoing)

## Purpose of the Study
- To develop my own skills in candidate selection as a Semester 1 MBA student
- To learn how to design and use selection tools: job description, competency framework, behavioral indicators, scoring sheet and structured interview
- To help candidates improve their performance in selection for their summer internships in 2027

## Candidates
Candidates are MBA freshers in Logistics & Supply Chain Management with no prior internship experience. All are anonymized (Candidate A, Candidate B, ...) and take part with consent. The first pilot is Candidate A; more will be added over time.

## Target Role
Supply Chain & Logistics Operations Intern. See the [job description](jd/intern-jd.md).

## Scope and Limitations
Small pilot study. This is a learning project, not a validated selection tool.
